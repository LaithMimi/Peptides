Flat, matte, deliberately unfinished design sketch of a desktop e-commerce catalog webpage's first viewport (landscape, 1536x1024), NOT a photograph, NOT a poster, NOT a finished screenshot — a designer's early structural sketch.

LAYOUT SCAFFOLD, stated literally, top to bottom:
1. Thin top header bar spanning full width: left side has a wordmark treated as a die-cut pharmaceutical label plate reading "PEP CLUB" in precise small-caps serif letters, with a thin engraved rule underneath it reading "PREMIUM PEPTIDES" in smaller tracked caps. Right side of the header has three small greeked nav placeholders (short gray rounded-rect blocks) and a small circular language-toggle chip.
2. Below the header, a wide horizontal hero strip about one-fifth of the viewport height: a single large die-cut label shape (rounded rectangle with a small circular perforation/seal at its top-left corner, like a pharmaceutical vial label) containing the ONE real headline "Premium Peptides" in bold pharmaceutical-serif small caps, with two lines of greeked body text (indistinct gray horizontal bars standing in for copy) beneath it, and a small pill-shaped button placeholder.
3. Below the hero, the main content area: a grid of 6 product cards (2 rows of 3), each card drawn as an individual die-cut vial label: a rounded rectangle with a small circular cap/seal shape at the top, a hairline rule below the seal, a short line of bold small-caps serif greeked text (product name placeholder), a row of tiny monospace greeked characters styled as a batch/lot number, and one more thin greeked line beneath. Each label card has a very slight independent rotation (a few degrees), as if physically affixed to a pegboard/shelf background, with a faint drop shadow.
4. A thin footer strip at the very bottom styled like a printed compliance stamp/rubber-stamp rectangle with a dashed border, containing one line of small greeked caps text.

PALETTE AND MATERIAL: warm cream/aged-paper label stock as the card and background surface (not pure white — a soft warm off-white with faint paper texture), deep navy ink (near-black blue, like a fountain-pen navy) for all structural linework, rules, and headline type, one muted teal-blue accent color used sparingly only for the seal/perforation circles and the button placeholder. No gradients, no glow, no neon, no glossy highlights.

TYPE CHARACTER: precise, exact, pharmaceutical/apothecary serif small-caps for headings and product names; a plain thin monospace for the batch/lot number lines; all body-copy regions are greeked as indistinct gray horizontal bars, never rendering invented words or claims.

STYLE: flat vector-like illustration, hard clean edges, visible ruled hairlines and grid structure, deliberately unfinished and sketch-like (not photorealistic, not glossy, no 3D lighting, no photography). This must read unmistakably as a webpage design sketch with real UI regions, not as a still-life photo of vials or labels.
