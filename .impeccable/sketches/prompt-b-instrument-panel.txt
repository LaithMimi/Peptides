Flat, matte, deliberately unfinished design sketch of a desktop e-commerce catalog webpage's first viewport (landscape, 1536x1024), NOT a photograph, NOT a poster, NOT a finished screenshot — a designer's early structural sketch.

LAYOUT SCAFFOLD, stated literally, top to bottom:
1. Thin top header bar spanning full width on a matte near-black panel ground: left side has a wordmark set in terse aviation-placard caps reading "PEP CLUB", with "PREMIUM PEPTIDES" beneath it in smaller tracked placard caps. Right side of the header has three small greeked nav placeholders (short thin rectangular blocks) and a small circular language-toggle chip, all rendered as tiny illuminated placards.
2. Below the header, a wide horizontal strip: the ONE real headline "Premium Peptides" set in bold white placard caps at left, with two lines of greeked body text (indistinct thin gray-white horizontal bars) beneath it, and one small pill-shaped button placeholder with a thin luminous outline.
3. Below that, the main content area: a grid of 6 circular gauge-face product cards (2 rows of 3) on the dark panel, each drawn as a round instrument dial: a circular bezel with tick marks around the rim, a short needle-like accent line inside pointing to one tick, a line of small bold placard-caps greeked text beneath the dial (product name placeholder), and one smaller line of terse greeked caps beneath that. Every dial face is near-black with luminous thin white tick marks and one glowing blue accent needle/marking per dial, evenly spaced in a fixed two-row grid, no rotation, no overlap — precise instrument-panel alignment.
4. A thin footer strip at the very bottom styled like an illuminated caution placard with a thin rule border, containing one line of small greeked caps text in amber.

PALETTE AND MATERIAL: matte near-black panel ground throughout, luminous white markings and numerals, one glowing blue accent color (used only for needles, active-state ticks, and the button outline) matching a deep-navy/blue tech brand color, plus a single small amber accent reserved only for the footer caution strip. No gradients on large surfaces beyond a very subtle vignette, no neon glow bloom, no glossy highlights, no photorealistic metal or glass reflections.

TYPE CHARACTER: condensed aviation/instrument placard caps for all headings and labels, tightly tracked; all body-copy regions are greeked as indistinct thin gray-white horizontal bars, never rendering invented words or claims.

STYLE: flat vector-like illustration, hard clean edges, visible instrument-panel grid structure, deliberately unfinished and sketch-like (not photorealistic, not glossy, no 3D lighting, no photography). This must read unmistakably as a webpage design sketch with real UI regions, not as a photo of an actual aircraft cockpit.
